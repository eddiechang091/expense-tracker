# Privacy Policy — Money Companion

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Money Companion ("the app") is a personal expense tracker. This policy
explains what data the app handles and where it goes. It is written in
plain language on purpose.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter is stored **privately per user** through Anna
Storage (the Anna platform's key-value store, scoped to your signed-in
account and this app). This includes:

- Expenses, categories, and budgets you log
- Your profile (display name, avatar choice, status)
- App settings (theme, currency preferences)
- Lucky-cat companion state (happiness, inventory, check-in streaks)
- Your Fish Audio voice reference ID (if you set one)

## What leaves your account

Two features send data to outside services, only when you use them:

1. **Money Buddy AI insights** — your expense summaries are sent to
   Anna's AI service to generate spending explanations. This is how the
   insight feature works; nothing is sent otherwise.
2. **Voice read-aloud (Fish Audio)** — when you ask the lucky cat to
   speak, the insight text is sent to Fish Audio (fish.audio) for speech
   synthesis. If you configured your own Fish API key, it is sent only to
   Fish Audio at synthesis time and never displayed or shared elsewhere.

## What the app does NOT do

- No advertising, no analytics trackers, no data brokers.
- Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.
- No bank connections, no account syncing, no financial advice.
- The app does not access your contacts, location, or files.

## Your control

- You can edit or delete your expenses, budgets, and profile inside the
  app at any time.
- Deleting the app removes its access to your stored data. To request
  deletion of data held by the platform, contact Anna support.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the new version will be
published here with an updated date.
